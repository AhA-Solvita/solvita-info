Tax 

Tax support built around your decisions — clear guidance, precise planning and a forward view that helps you make well‑informed, profitable choices.

Scope of Work

Business Tax

  • Corporation Tax (CT600)
  • VAT registration and returns
  • VAT adjustments and ongoing compliance
  • CIS deductions and reporting
  • Tax implications of business changes (new activity, property flip, restructuring)
  • Monitoring thresholds and statutory deadlines
  • Preparing structured responses for HMRC enquiries and checks

Personal Tax for Business Owners

  • Self‑Assessment (SA100)
  • Income Tax planning for business owners and directors
  • Tax planning aligned with the owner’s personal tax position
  • Dividends, salary, benefits and withdrawals
  • Inheritance Tax considerations where relevant

Cross‑Border & International Tax

  • UK tax considerations for clients with income or assets abroad
  • Income or gains arising from overseas assets
  • Rental income from overseas properties
  • Capital gains on the sale of overseas assets
  • Double taxation and foreign tax credit relief
  • Clear explanations for clients with international ties

Property‑Related Taxes

  • Council Tax and Business Rates guidance for property investors
  • Tax treatment of property income and expenses
  • Tax implications of property flips and developments
  • Capital allowances and allowable costs
  • Tax alignment for SPVs and property groups

Customs, Duties & Import Taxes

  • Guidance on customs duties and import VAT
  • Classification and documentation support
  • Preparing structured explanations for HMRC
  • Ensuring correct accounting treatment of duties and import VAT
  • Support for businesses importing goods into the UK

Tax Planning & Structuring

  • Tax‑efficient structuring for micro and small companies
  • Alignment between business structure, personal tax and accounting
  • Group and holding structure tax alignment
  • Timing of income and expenses
  • Allowances, reliefs and exemptions

Ownership & Income Allocation

  • Reviewing beneficial ownership for tax purposes
  • Adjusting allocation of property income between spouses or partners
  • Preparing evidence and explanations for HMRC
  • Ensuring correct tax treatment based on actual entitlement
  • Aligning ownership structure with personal and business tax planning
