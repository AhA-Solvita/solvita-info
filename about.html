About Solvita

Solvita is a London‑based consultancy providing integrated legal services within the permitted non‑reserved scope, alongside tax, accounting and compliance support for clients across the UK and internationally. The service is fully remote and designed for people who value clarity, structure and continuity as their work develops.

We bring together areas that are often handled by separate firms, keeping your tax position, accounts, registrations, filings and legal documents aligned as one coherent system. This removes fragmentation and gives you support that is connected, consistent and built around the way you work.

Who we work with

We support individuals and businesses across a wide range of sectors, from sole traders and limited companies to partnerships, charities and property clients.

About the Founder

Solvita is led by Anna Andersen, a legal and governance professional with experience across the private and non - government sectors. 

Her background combines legal training with structured study in accounting and taxation, together with practical experience in managing organisations and overseeing their statutory, financial and governance responsibilities.

This blend of legal insight and operational experience shapes a structured, analytical and systems‑driven approach to supporting businesses as they stabilise, grow and meet their statutory and financial responsibilities.
