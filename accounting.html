Accounting 

Accounting that brings clarity and continuity — accurate records, steady oversight and stability that keeps your entire structure in order.

Bookkeeping & Financial Records

  • Full bookkeeping for micro and small companies, sole traders and partnerships
  • Bookkeeping support for clients who enter their own data
  • Setting up the accounting system and workflow
  • Periodic reviews and corrections to ensure accuracy
  • Bookkeeping clearance before annual accounts
  • Ensuring records are complete, consistent and ready for annual accounts

Accounts Preparation

  • Annual accounts for micro and small companies
  • Receipts & payments or accruals accounts for charities
  • Adjusting entries and year‑end corrections
  • Ensuring alignment between accounting, tax and business decisions
  • Preparing supporting schedules and documentation
  • Reviewing the business profile (e.g., property flip, new service, new income stream) and adjusting accounting treatment accordingly

Tax Filings

  • Corporation Tax
  • VAT registration and returns
  • VAT adjustments and ongoing compliance
  • PAYE payroll and RTI submissions
  • Construction Industry Scheme deductions and reporting
  • Self‑Assessment for directors and business owners

Cost Classification & Financial Accuracy

  • Correct cost classification throughout the year
  • Ensuring expenses match the business model and tax rules
  • Identifying misclassifications and correcting them before year‑end
  • Ensuring clean, audit‑ready records

Reviews, Checks & Clarifications

  • Preparing documents for reviews, checks and clarifications
  • Ensuring accounting records match tax and compliance requirements
  • Providing explanations and evidence for HMRC or lenders
  • Ensuring financial documentation is consistent with governance and resolutions

Accounting System Choice & Support

  • Advising whether to use your own accounting setup or leave the full process with us
  • Helping clients choose the right system for their business
  • Setting up chart of accounts, workflows and controls
  • Providing ongoing support and periodic audits of client‑maintained systems

Charities & Not‑for‑Profit (Small and Medium)

  • Bookkeeping and financial records
  • Receipts & payments or accruals accounts
  • Annual accounts and supporting documentation
  • Preparing information for Charity Commission submissions
  • Bookkeeping clearance and system setup
  • Ensuring alignment with governance, tax and accounting
  • Support for trustees and small charity committees
