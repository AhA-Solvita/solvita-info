Compliance

Compliance support that protects your operations — clear structure, aligned processes and steady regulatory support that keeps your business stable.

Scope of Work

Governance & Corporate Structure

  • Company governance for micro and small businesses
  • Compliance for holding structures and group entities
  • PSC management and statutory registers
  • Resolutions and minutes for business decisions
  • Ensuring decisions are aligned with tax and accounting treatment
  • Director duties and decision‑making frameworks
  • Ensuring alignment between governance, tax and accounting

Companies House & Corporate Compliance

  • Maintaining statutory registers and PSC records
  • Preparing resolutions and minutes for business decisions
  • Filing statutory changes (directors, shareholders, addresses, share structure)
  • Preparing confirmation statements and supporting documents
  • Ensuring company records match real operations 
  • Reviewing company structure for compliance and risk
  • Coordinating filings across multi‑entity and holding structures
  • Ensuring Companies House documentation is consistent with governance, tax and accounting

Operational Compliance

  • Operational policies and procedures tailored to the business
  • Internal controls and workflow documentation
  • Compliance processes for day‑to‑day operations
  • Updating compliance when business activities change
  • Ensuring documentation matches real operations 

Risk & Internal Controls

  • Business‑level risk assessments
  • Identifying operational, financial and compliance risks
  • Designing proportionate internal controls
  • Ongoing monitoring of regulatory obligations
  • Practical guidance for reducing exposure to penalties and disputes

AML & Due Diligence

  • AML documentation and internal processes
  • Customer due diligence procedures 
  • AML registers and evidence files
  • Risk‑based approach for onboarding clients
  • Preparing for AML checks and audits
  • Clarifying AML requirements for specific business models

Regulatory Documentation & Reporting

  • Mandatory reporting requirements for small companies
  • Preparing compliance documentation for regulators
  • Clarifying regulatory expectations and obligations
  • Responding to compliance checks and enquiries
  • Evidence packs, explanations and structured responses

Entity Management & SPV Compliance

  • Compliance for property SPVs
  • Documentation for investors and shareholders
  • Maintaining statutory records and filings
  • Ensuring SPV alignment with tax and accounting
  • Ongoing monitoring of obligations for multi‑entity structures

Business‑Specific Obligations & Insurance Requirements

  • Identifying mandatory insurance requirements for specific business types
  • Clarifying statutory obligations for landlords, traders and service providers
  • Ensuring business activities meet minimum legal and regulatory standards
  • Preparing documentation and evidence for compliance checks
  • Aligning operational obligations with tax, accounting and governance
  • Highlighting sector‑specific risks and required internal controls

Charity Governance & Compliance (Small and Medium)

  • Governance support for trustees and committees
  • Policies, procedures and internal controls
  • Minutes, resolutions and decision‑making documentation
  • Ensuring alignment between governance, accounting and reporting
  • Preparing documentation for Charity Commission submissions
  • Risk assessments and operational compliance for small charities
