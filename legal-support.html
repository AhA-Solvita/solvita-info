Legal

Legal support that protects your position — clear agreements, precise drafting, and structured, effective preparation with consistent guidance at every stage.

Scope of Work

Commercial & Business

  • Drafting and reviewing commercial agreements
  • Drafting service agreements
  • Preparing terms, policies and operational documents
  • Contract drafting and clause refinement
  • Reviewing client-provided documents prior to signature
  • Preparing payment demands and receivables documentation
  • Negotiating with counterparties

Security & Debt Protection

  • Preparing loan agreements and promissory notes
  • Drafting personal guarantees
  • Drafting contractual security clauses
  • Preparing equitable charges (non‑registered security)
  • Preparing notices of assignment and notices of charge
  • Drafting retention of title clauses
  • Preparing acknowledgements of debt
  • Structuring repayment plans
  • Preparing evidence and documentation for enforcement procedures

Enforcement & Charging Orders

  • Preparing applications for charging orders (post‑CCJ)
  • Drafting interim and final charging order documents
  • Preparing Land Registry documentation for court‑ordered security
  • Preparing notices to debtors and co‑owners
  • Preparing schedules of debt and evidence packs

Disputes & Court Procedure

  • Preparing pre‑action strategy
  • Drafting letters before action and responses
  • Providing procedural guidance (applications, statements, evidence, timelines)
  • Supporting litigants in person step‑by‑step
  • Drafting applications for injunctions (where appropriate)
  • Preparing bundles, schedules and supporting documents
  • Coordinating with solicitors or barristers
  • Providing enforcement support for creditors and debtors

HMRC‑Related Legal Support

  • Preparing challenges to HMRC decisions
  • Drafting structured responses to HMRC enquiries
  • Preparing evidence, explanations and supporting documents
  • Negotiating with HMRC where appropriate
  • Ensuring legal alignment with tax and accounting positions

Property Legal Support

  • Drafting residential and commercial tenancy agreements
  • Reviewing leases and identifying risks
  • Supporting landlord–tenant matters
  • Preparing notices, demands and procedural documentation
  • Coordinating with solicitors and lenders
  • Supporting property transactions (documents, checks, timelines)
